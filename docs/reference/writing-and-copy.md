# Writing and Copy Reference

Source:
- WRITING.md: https://github.com/Anbeeld/WRITING.md/blob/main/WRITING.md

Use this reference for Fluso website copy, product copy, docs, announcements, and design-system prose.

## Core Idea

Write for the actual context. Do not optimize for "sounding human". Do not write generic AI-flavored prose. Fit the medium, the reader, and the job of the text.

## Precedence

1. Truth, safety, accessibility, legal and platform requirements.
2. Explicit user direction.
3. Genre and medium norms.
4. Core writing rules.
5. Optional style heuristics.

## Workflow

1. Identify the medium, audience, reader need, and job of the text.
2. Put the answer or next action early when the copy is task-oriented.
3. For longer pieces, choose one through-line and one concrete example.
4. Draft for the context, not for a generic idea of "good writing".
5. Cut generic, ceremonial, over-engineered, or suspiciously tidy prose.
6. Check fragile facts before keeping them.

## Fluso Voice

Fluso should sound calm, exact, and grounded.

Use:
- Plain words.
- Specific claims.
- Concrete product consequences.
- Short, direct CTAs.
- Human warmth without sentimentality.
- Confidence when evidence is strong.

Avoid:
- Generic AI category language.
- Over-polished mission statements.
- Unsupported claims about trust, security, speed, or outcomes.
- Canned phrases like "unlock", "empower", "seamless", "robust", "pivotal", "ever-evolving".
- Decorative punctuation patterns that repeat across paragraphs.
- Three-part cadence used by reflex.

## Website Copy Rules

- Hero copy should be literal before poetic.
- The headline should say what Fluso is or what category it owns.
- The subheadline should add one concrete reason to believe.
- CTA labels should be simple and action-oriented.
- Product page copy should show observable consequences, not abstract benefits.
- Docs and help copy should put the answer first.
- Design-system copy can be editorial, but still needs concrete usage guidance.

## Revision Checks

Run these before shipping important copy:

1. Register fit
   - Does the structure match the medium?
   - Does the copy sound like the page it lives on?

2. Concrete-anchor audit
   - Does each substantial paragraph contain a checkable detail, product behavior, number, decision, example, or observed consequence?

3. Fact discipline
   - Are dates, numbers, quotes, roadmap claims, and causal claims verified?

4. Source-fit check
   - Does the source support the exact claim, not just a nearby topic?

5. Regularity tripwire
   - Is the same sentence shape, punctuation move, or paragraph rhythm repeating?

6. Stance and voice
   - Does the genre need a view, or should it stay neutral?

7. Over-correction
   - Did we add fake casualness, slang, typos, or forced messiness?

## Useful Transformations

Generic:
"Fluso empowers teams to unlock the full potential of AI."

Better:
"Fluso gives teams a proactive AI workspace for real projects, documents, and decisions."

Generic:
"A seamless platform for modern workflows."

Better:
"One place to build, review, and ship AI-assisted work without scattering context."

Generic:
"Built for trust and security."

Better:
"Designed for private workspaces, controlled access, and clear handoffs."

Generic:
"Transform how your team works."

Better:
"Bring your team's AI work into one structured place."
