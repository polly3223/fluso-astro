Fluso
Product one-pager
Partners / customers / investors

AI workspaces for teams with too much context.

Fluso connects to your work apps, remembers project history, and turns scattered communication into tasks, research, files, and finished work. It is built for organizations where the hard part is not generating text - it is knowing what happened, what matters, and what needs to happen next.

Core capabilities

The practical feature set: connect, understand, act, and return with the right context.

01

Connected work context

Search and use Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Slack, GitHub, Linear, web research, files, meetings, and project history.

02

Business context memory

A private operating memory of people, projects, decisions, files, meetings, deadlines, and source-linked evidence.

03

Proactive task detection

Finds open loops in messages and meetings: follow-ups, approvals, waiting items, deadlines, and recurring work.

04

Custom skills

Package repeatable team workflows for research, reporting, analysis, follow-ups, operations, and specialist work.

05
DOC PDF XLS PPT MD

Artifact generation

Creates research briefs, reports, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, drafts, tickets, and project files.

06

Automations

Schedules recurring checks, summaries, reminders, meeting follow-ups, monitoring, and preparation workflows.
